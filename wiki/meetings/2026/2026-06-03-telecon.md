## [animation-triggers-1] Should `play-forwards`/`play-backwards` no-op at the end? ([#13643](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13643))

Reworks the play actions: they no longer auto-rewind at the end; a new `play-always` does, and
`play-once` is removed as redundant.

> RESOLVED: play, play-f, play-b do not auto-rewind at the end; we add play-always which does; we remove play-once since that's now 'play' behavior
