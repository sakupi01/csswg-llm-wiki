## [css-anchor-position-1] Nature of the `@position-try` tree-scoped name ([#13567](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13567))

Whether `@position-try` names are tree-scoped (like most CSS names) or global. The group resolved
they are **globally scoped, like `@keyframes`**. Related: [anchor positioning](../../features/anchor-positioning.md).

> RESOLVED: @position-try uses a globally scoped name like @keyframes

([resolution](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13567#issuecomment-4791554191))

## [css-values-5] `sibling-index()`/`count()` on a failed tree reference ([#13736](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13736))

Edge-case for the new tree-counting functions: what they return when there is nothing to count.
The group chose 1 (not 0) to avoid division-by-zero surprises in typical `calc()` usage.

> RESOLVED: if sibling-index() or sibling-count() fails to find a thing to count, it will return 1

([resolution](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13736#issuecomment-4791604896))
