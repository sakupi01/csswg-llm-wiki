## [web-animations][scroll-animations] Animations on inactive timelines ([#9256](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/9256))

Whether scroll/view timelines with no scroll range are "inactive." The group decided they are not.
Related: [scroll-driven animations](../../features/scroll-driven-animations.md).

> RESOLVED: Scroll and view timelines don't become inactive when there's no scroll range

([resolution](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/9256#issuecomment-4556112966))

## [css-align] Fallback alignment for baseline self-alignment ([#13539](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13539))
