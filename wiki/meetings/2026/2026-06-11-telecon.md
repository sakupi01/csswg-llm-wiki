## [a11y] Should `::interest-button` be opt-in, opt-out, or media-query gated? ([#13980](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/13980))

The group decided to gate the `::interest-button` pseudo-element behind an inline media condition
so it only appears where appropriate (e.g. touchscreens). This resolution was **voided two weeks
later** ([2026-06-25](2026-06-25-telecon.md)).

> RESOLVED: use an inline media condition (display: if(media())) to enable the pseudo element on systems where it makes sense, such as touchscreen.
